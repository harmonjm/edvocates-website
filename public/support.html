Getting started

A quick tour of Keepr

The Planner shows each kid’s missed work, what’s due next, and what they’ve already checked off. Switch kids with the buttons on top. Grades has their GPA and every class, Messages keeps every conversation, and Settings is where alerts and family sharing live.

Connect your school’s Canvas (Keepr Parent)

Tap Connect Canvas, type your school’s Canvas address, and tap Get a token from Canvas. Sign in with your Canvas parent login. Tap New Access Token, name it Keepr, pick a date, and tap Generate Token. Select the whole token, tap Copy, then tap Paste at the bottom and tap Continue. Keepr never sees your password.

Connect your school’s Canvas (Keepr Student)

Same steps, signed in as the student: tap Connect Canvas, type your school’s Canvas address, and tap Get a token from Canvas. Sign in with your own Canvas student login, make a new access token named Keepr, copy it, tap Paste, and tap Connect.

The Planner

When your kid says they’re taking the zero

If your kid marks something “Not turning it in” in Keepr Student, it shows up on your planner with OK and Not OK. Tap OK and it moves to Settled on both phones. Tap Not OK and Keepr opens a message about it. Haven’t answered by the next day? Keepr reminds you.

Check something off (Keepr Student)

Tap the box next to anything on your planner and pick how it got done: in Canvas, on paper, excused, or you talked to your teacher. It stays crossed off today and goes away tomorrow. Not done yet? Tap ⋯ for a reminder, or to plan a talk with your teacher.

High Fives and streaks

The scoreboard above the planner counts high fives, the homework streak (school days in a row it all got turned in), and the run of tests at a B or better. Tap the fire or the star for every day of a streak. Tap Got it and it folds into the pill next to the planner’s name; tap the pill to bring it back.

The quarter calendar

Tap “Q1 closes in 17 days” at the top of the Planner. Dots mark tests, big work, and missing work. Tap a day to see what was due, use the arrows for other months, and tap End of Q1 to jump to the quarter’s last day.

Family sharing

Add the other parent

Open Settings, scroll to Sharing with Family, and tap Share with family. Send the invite however you like. Once they open it on their iPhone and install Keepr Parent, they show up as a Parent. They connect their own Canvas parent account for grades.

Add your student

From the same spot, tap Add or remove people and invite your kid. They open the invite in Keepr Student and show up as a Student. From then on, what they check off shows up on your planner, and they can answer your messages in Keepr.

Messages

Send a message about missed work

Tap Message on any planner section and pick the lines it’s about. Keepr builds a card with them. Add a note, or tap Write a draft, then send it. If your kid has joined your family share, tap Send in Keepr and the reply comes back to the Messages tab, under your question. Otherwise tap Open in Messages and send it as a text. Lots of old missed work? Tap Message about all.

Answer in one tap, or take it back

Under a reply, tap 👍, Help?, Update?, or Talk tonight to answer without typing. Sent the wrong one? Tap Delete within 15 minutes and it’s gone for everyone.

Notifications

Choose what pings you, and when

Keepr checks Canvas in the background and tells you when a grade drops, work goes missing, a zero comes back, or your kid replies. In Settings → Notifications, pick Just the big stuff, Daily digests (a before-school note and an end-of-day list), or Tell me everything. Tap Fine-tune to choose each alert, when a class gets flagged, the digest times, and quiet hours.

Fixing things

Keepr stopped updating

Canvas tokens can expire or get revoked. If a school says Fix instead of Manage, tap it and choose Update token. Make a new token in Canvas, paste it in, and your kids and settings stay just as they were.

Trial & subscription

What does it cost?

Keepr Parent is free for 14 days, then $5.99 a month or $39.99 a year. Keepr Student is free. You can explore with sample data for free, anytime.

How do I start the trial?

Tap Connect Canvas and choose a plan. Apple asks you to confirm, and you won’t be charged until the 14 days are up.

How do I cancel?

In Keepr, open Settings → Subscription → Manage subscription. You can also cancel in the iPhone Settings app under your name → Subscriptions. Cancel at least a day before the trial ends to avoid being charged.

I got a new phone

Open Settings → Subscription and tap Restore Purchases. Then connect Canvas again with a new token.

Does the other parent need to pay?

No. Someone you invite to family sharing can use it without their own subscription, and Family Sharing in the App Store can cover the whole household.

Remove someone or stop sharing

The parent who set up sharing can tap Remove next to anyone in Settings → Sharing with Family, or tap Stop sharing. Anyone else can tap Leave this share.
