Getting started

A quick tour of Keepr

Home shows what needs a look for every kid. Kids has each class, Messages keeps every conversation, and Settings is where alerts and family sharing live.

Connect your school’s Canvas

Tap Connect Canvas and type your school’s Canvas address. Keepr opens Canvas so you can sign in and make an access token (Account → Settings → New Access Token). Copy it, come back, paste it in, and tap Continue. Keepr never sees your password.

Family sharing

Add the other parent

Open Settings, scroll to Sharing with Family, and tap Share with family. Send the invite however you like. Once they open it on their iPhone and install Keepr Parent, they show up as a Parent. They connect their own Canvas parent account for grades.

Add your student

From the same spot, tap Add or remove people and invite your kid. They open the invite in Keepr Student and show up as a Student. On their phone, they see the whole family in Settings.

Messages

Send a text about a grade

Tap Text Maya (or Follow up) in Needs your attention. Keepr builds a card with the grades and missing work. Add a note, or tap Write a draft, then tap Open in Messages and pick who to send it to.

Send a message in Keepr

Once your kid has joined your family share, the send sheet offers “Maya is on Keepr.” Tap Send in Keepr and the message stays in the app. The reply comes back to the Messages tab, under your question.

Answer in one tap, or take it back

Under a reply, tap 👍, Help?, Update?, or Talk tonight to answer without typing. Sent the wrong one? Tap Delete within 15 minutes and it’s gone for everyone.

Notifications

How notifications work

Keepr checks Canvas in the background and taps you on the shoulder when a grade drops, work goes missing, a zero comes back, or your kid replies. Tap a notification to jump straight to it.

Choose what pings you, and when

In Settings, pick which alerts you want, whether a class is flagged below a B, below a C, or only when failing, and when you hear about it: right away, or in a before-school note and an end-of-day recap. Quiet hours holds everything until they’re over.

Fixing things

Keepr stopped updating

Canvas tokens can expire or get revoked. If a school says Fix instead of Manage, tap it and choose Update token. Make a new token in Canvas, paste it in, and your kids and settings stay just as they were.

Trial & subscription

What does it cost?

Keepr Parent is free for 14 days, then $5.99 a month or $39.99 a year. Keepr Student is free. You can look around with sample data for free, anytime.

How do I start the trial?

Tap Connect Canvas and choose a plan. Apple asks you to confirm, and you won’t be charged until the 14 days are up.

How do I cancel?

In Keepr, open Settings → Subscription → Manage subscription. You can also cancel in the iPhone Settings app under your name → Subscriptions. Cancel at least a day before the trial ends to avoid being charged.

I got a new phone

Open Settings → Subscription and tap Restore Purchases. Then connect Canvas again with a new token.

Does the other parent need to pay?

No. Someone you invite to family sharing can use it without their own subscription, and Family Sharing in the App Store can cover the whole household.

Remove someone or stop sharing

The parent who set up sharing can tap Remove next to anyone in Settings → Sharing with Family, or tap Stop sharing. Anyone else can tap Leave this share.
