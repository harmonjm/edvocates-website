Getting set up

Three steps. No homework.

  1. Connect Canvas

    Keepr walks you through making a Canvas access token, right inside the app. No password to hand over.

  2. Match your kids

    Keepr finds the students on your Canvas parent account. You tell it who’s who.

  3. Get on with your day

    Keepr keeps itself up to date and pings you when something needs a look.
