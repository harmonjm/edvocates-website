Getting set up

Three steps. No homework.

  1. Connect Canvas

    Start your 14-day free trial ($5.99 a month or $39.99 a year after that, cancel anytime), and Keepr walks you through making a Canvas access token in the app. No password to hand over.

  2. Match your kids

    Keepr finds the students on your Canvas parent account. Tell it who’s who, and what your kids call you.

  3. Bring the family

    Invite the other parent and your kid from Settings, so what your kid checks off shows up on your planner. Then get on with your day.
